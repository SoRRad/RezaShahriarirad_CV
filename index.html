Profile

About

Dr. Reza Shahriarirad is a physician-researcher and Research Fellow at the Surgery Innovation Center, Mayo Clinic, in Rochester, Minnesota. His work focuses on surgical innovation, minimally invasive surgery, and the application of data-driven and artificial intelligence-based approaches to improve surgical care.

His academic work includes more than 190 peer-reviewed publications, with contributions across surgical outcomes, thoracic and minimally invasive surgery, infectious diseases, burn care, and surgical AI. He has also contributed to editorial and peer-review activities for international journals and has been recognized among the World's Top 2% Scientists.

Ongoing Research at Mayo Clinic

Since joining the Surgery Innovation Center, Dr. Shahriarirad has been involved in over 30 IRB-approved research projects spanning a broad range of surgical specialties.
