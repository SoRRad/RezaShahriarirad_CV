About

Dr. Reza Shahriarirad is a physician-researcher and Research Fellow at the Surgery Innovation Center, Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota. His work spans minimally invasive surgery, artificial intelligence in medicine, and translational surgical research.

With over 193 peer-reviewed publications, an H-index of 24, Dr. Shahriarirad has established a prolific international research career. He has served as corresponding author on 68 publications, last/senior author on 50, and first author on 20.

He is an active peer reviewer for 67 international journals with 149 completed reviews, and has served as Guest Editor at PLOS One and Associate Editor at the Shiraz E-Medical Journal. His research interests include surgery, minimally invasive surgery, and artificial intelligence.
